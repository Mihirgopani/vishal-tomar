DATA HANDLING.

How personal information, case inquiries, and digital interactions are protected in accordance with professional legal conduct and the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023.

STATUTORY ADVOCATE-CLIENT PRIVILEGE · DPDP ACT, 2023 COMPLIANCE
FIDUCIARY DUTY & ETHICS CHAMBERS CONFIDENTIALITY PROTOCOL

"Advocate Vishal Tomar is committed to safeguarding the absolute privacy, statutory privilege, and commercial sensitivity of individuals and corporate entities interacting with these chambers. Legal advocacy demands uncompromised discretion, and our digital infrastructure reflects this ethical mandate."

01

Information Collected

We collect only information voluntarily submitted by you through our consultation request protocols, secure email communications, or direct chambers conferences:

  • Identification Data: Full name, designation, and corporate entity registration details.
  • Contact Coordinates: Verified business or personal email address and direct telephone contact.
  • Matter Synopsis: Preliminary non-sensitive factual context necessary for conflict clearance.
02

Purpose of Processing

Your information is processed strictly in accordance with ethical practice and legitimate professional ends:

  • Conducting mandatory conflict-of-interest checks before undertaking representation;
  • Scheduling confidential chambers consultations or High Court strategy conferences;
  • Responding directly to specific statutory, writ, or arbitration enquiries; and
  • Complying with statutory documentation retention requirements under Indian law.
03

Client Privilege & Non-Disclosure

We do not sell, rent, commercialize, or share client information with marketing third parties. Once an advocate-client relationship is formally constituted through a Vakalatnama or retainer agreement, all communications and work products are protected under statutory professional privilege as recognized under Indian law.

04

Cookies & Local Storage

This website does not utilize commercial advertising pixels or behavioral profiling cookies. We use browser localStorage solely to remember user consent regarding the Bar Council of India regulatory disclaimer, avoiding intrusive modal prompts on subsequent page transitions.

05

Data Retention & Registry Inquiries

Unretained preliminary queries that do not proceed to a formal engagement are securely deleted after periodic administrative audits. Inquiries regarding records retention or data queries may be directed to chambers administration at counsel@vishaltomar.in.

Privacy & Data Ethics Affirmation
