Industry Landscape

Dispute Resolution in Major Projects

Gujarat’s ambitious infrastructure blueprint—ranging from expressways, metro rail networks, and renewable energy parks to deep-water ports—operates within rigid statutory and contractual frameworks.

In large-scale EPC and concession projects, legal disputes frequently threaten business continuity: sudden termination notices, wrongful encashment of unconditional bank guarantees, and denial of time-extension claims. When project owners are government corporations or statutory boards, conventional litigation often proves inadequate without immediate High Court intervention.

Advocate Vishalkumar R Tomar provides comprehensive dispute counsel to infrastructure developers, primary contractors, and engineering consortia. Our strategic interventions focus on securing immediate interim injunctions against bank guarantee forfeiture, challenging arbitrary tender disqualifications through Article 226 writs, and conducting disciplined commercial arbitrations.

"In EPC disputes, success depends upon contemporaneous project correspondence—establishing employer delays, site handover failures, and design changes to defeat liquidated damage counterclaims."

Dispute Typology

Critical Vulnerabilities & Strategic Responses

Common legal points of friction in Gujarat infrastructure contracts and the advocate's tactical approach.

Bank Guarantee Invocations

When project authorities attempt to encash performance or advance guarantees despite employer breach, we file urgent Section 9 Arbitration petitions or Special Civil Applications establishing egregious fraud or irretrievable harm.

Delay Claims & Prolongation Costs

Building forensic claim dossiers for idle plant machinery, overhead prolongation, and price variation (escalation clauses) resulting from unhindered site handover defaults by state authorities.

Tender Rejections & Disqualification

Filing High Court writs challenging arbitrary rejection of technical bids, restrictive eligibility modifications after submission, and non-transparent tender awards violating Article 14.

Blacklisting & Debarment Orders

Challenging unconstitutional debarment and blacklisting notices issued without proper show-cause notices or proportionality, securing urgent stays to preserve bidding eligibility.

Renewable Energy PPA Litigations

Representation before the Gujarat Electricity Regulatory Commission (GERC) and Appellate Tribunal for Electricity (APTEL) regarding tariff adoption, change-in-law compensations, and curtailment.

Arbitrator Appointment & Award Enforcement

Filing Section 11 petitions before the Gujarat High Court to overcome unilateral arbitrator appointment clauses and conducting Section 36 execution proceedings for domestic arbitral awards.

Sample Project Dispute Protocol

Contemporaneous Audit

Review of monthly progress reports, RFI submissions, site obstruction notices, and contractual notice requirements under the contract.

02

Emergency Injunction

03

Dispute Notice & S.11

Issuance of formal statutory notice invoking the arbitration clause and filing Section 11 petition for independent judicial arbitrator appointment.

Submission of Statement of Claim with expert engineering delay analysis, cross-examination of project managers, and enforcement of award.
