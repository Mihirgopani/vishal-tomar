01

Non-Solicitation Principle

Under Rule 36 of Chapter II, Part VI of the Bar Council of India Rules, an advocate is strictly prohibited from soliciting work or advertising, either directly or indirectly, whether by circulars, advertisements, touts, personal communications, or interviews. This website is hosted solely to provide general factual information concerning the legal practice, jurisdiction, credentials, and professional publications of Advocate Vishal Tomar upon specific user request.

02

No Attorney-Client Privilege or Retainer Creation

The contents of this platform—including statutory analyses, judicial precedents, practice notes, and articles—are published for general informational awareness only. None of the material constitutes legal opinion, formal legal advice, or a recommendation for any specific litigation course.

Accessing, reviewing, or submitting preliminary details through this website does not constitute an advocate-client relationship. A formal legal relationship is constituted only upon:

  • Conducting an adversarial conflict-of-interest audit against opposing parties;
  • Execution of a formal Vakalatnama or written professional engagement agreement; and
  • Mutual agreement regarding fee schedules and scope of courtroom representation.
03

Confidentiality & Statutory Privilege Notice

While all communications are handled with discretion, unsolicited case facts or records transmitted electronically prior to a formal retainer may not be protected by statutory attorney-client privilege under Section 126 of the Indian Evidence Act / Section 132 of the Bharatiya Sakshya Adhiniyam, 2023. Litigants are advised to refrain from sending highly sensitive commercial trade secrets or trial exhibits prior to execution of formal representation agreements.

04

Accuracy & Informational Warranty

While Advocate Vishal Tomar endeavors to ensure statutory accuracy and precedential currency, Indian jurisprudence and High Court interpretations evolve continuously. No warranty, express or implied, is given concerning the absolute completeness or continuing applicability of digital materials. Advocate Vishal Tomar expressly disclaims liability for actions taken in reliance upon general website summaries.

05

Governing Law & Exclusive Territorial Jurisdiction

Any disputes or legal questions arising from or connected to the use of this digital portal shall be governed exclusively by the laws of the Republic of India. The competent judicial courts and tribunals situated in Ahmedabad, Gujarat, India shall have exclusive territorial and subject-matter jurisdiction.
